iPhone tip: if the Photos picker closes without adding the item, open index.html in Safari (not the Files document preview).

NOIR preserves the original dark grade, muted cool tone, vignette and film grain. Video exports are rendered at about 1.5× speed with deliberately reduced frame smoothness. On iPhone, Safari may export video without audio because its local MediaRecorder APIs do not reliably expose source audio; the app reports that limitation instead of leaving processing stuck.
